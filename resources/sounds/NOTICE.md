**What was changed.** `kia-open` is the 8.8s–20.9s span of the film's audio,
level-matched with a short fade at each end. `kia-settle` is the 9.0s–19.0s span
replayed at half speed (an octave down), high-passed at 60Hz, low-passed at
700Hz, put through a synthetic reverb, and closed into a seamless 13-second loop
by folding its tail back over its head with an equal-power crossfade.
